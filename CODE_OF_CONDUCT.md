# Community conduct

Participate respectfully. Critique ideas and software with concrete evidence.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deceptive links and spam are
not permitted. Good-faith criticism and security reporting are welcome.

Use GitHub's report-content controls for violations. Do not repeat private or
harmful information in a public report. Maintainers review context and may hide
content, ask for edits, lock a disruptive thread or apply temporary restrictions.
Clear spam may be hidden immediately; ambiguous cases require human review.

Moderation should be proportionate, documented and reversible where possible.
A person affected may request review through the repository's available GitHub
reporting channels. Do not disclose private incident records publicly.
